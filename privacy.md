# Privacy Policy for Clip Land

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Clip Land ("we", "us", or "the App") handles information when you use the desktop application.

## 1. Information We Collect
**Clip Land is a local desktop application.**  
It processes your videos entirely on your device. We do **not** collect, transmit, or store your videos, personal data, or usage information on any servers controlled by us.

The App does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track your usage for analytics

## 2. Local Processing
All video conversion happens locally on your computer. Your files never leave your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The current version of Clip Land does not integrate with third-party services that collect data. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children.
